Our Story

We Reimagined
What PG Living
Could Be.

Galactic Living was born out of frustration. Our founders experienced the broken reality of traditional PGs firsthand — bad food, poor security, zero community, and the feeling of paying too much for too little.

They set out to build something radically different: a PG that felt like a real home, not a hostel with WiFi. Since 2021, Galactic Living has served 500+ residents across 6 premium properties in Ahmedabad.

  • Three chef-prepared meals included daily
  • Hotel-grade housekeeping as standard
  • Biometric security & CCTV throughout
  • Community events and curated resident programs
  • Transparent pricing — no hidden fees
